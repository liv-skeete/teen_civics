## Summary in plain English

TeenCivics is a civics education site. We summarize Congressional
bills, let you vote in informal polls, and award participation points
called "Votes" that move you up a tier ladder. By using the site you
agree to:

- Tell the truth (don't impersonate other people)
- Be 13 or older
- Not abuse the site (no bots, no spam, no harassment)
- Accept that the polls are non-binding — we don't send your vote to
  Congress
- Accept that we may suspend or close your account if you break these
  rules

We agree to:

- Run the site honestly, not show ads, not sell your data
- Keep your account info private (see Privacy Policy)
- Tell you 30 days in advance if these terms change materially
- Let you delete your account whenever

## Eligibility

You must be at least **13 years old** to use TeenCivics. If you are
under 18, your parent or guardian is encouraged to review these
terms with you.

We may suspend or terminate accounts we believe are operated by users
under 13. If you become aware that an under-13 user has registered,
please email contact@teencivics.org.

## Your account

- Sign up using your real email or your Google account
- One person per account; one account per person
- You're responsible for keeping your account secure (don't share
  your magic-link emails or your Google credentials)
- If you suspect unauthorized access, email
  contact@teencivics.org immediately
- You can delete your account at any time via `/account/delete`

## What you may do

- Read bill summaries, vote in polls, earn Votes and climb tiers
- Use the site for personal, non-commercial civic education
- Cite our content with attribution
- Embed/share individual bill pages on social media (we'd love that)

## What you may NOT do

- Run bots, scrapers, or automated voting tools that attempt to bypass
  our rate limits or signup requirements. **Bots may freely read
  public pages** but writes (votes, account creation, profile edits)
  are reserved for verified humans.
- Create accounts with disposable email addresses for the purpose of
  inflating vote counts or tier progression
- Impersonate other users, public figures, or organizations
- Post threatening, harassing, hateful, sexually explicit, or
  defamatory content in any user-editable field (display name, bio)
- Attempt to compromise the security of the site (penetration testing
  without authorization, etc.)
- Use the site to spread disinformation or coordinate harassment
- Resell or sublicense TeenCivics content

## The polls are informal

TeenCivics community polls **are not binding votes** and are not
transmitted to Congress. The "Tell Your Rep" feature drafts a
message you can choose to send to your actual representative, but
TeenCivics does not send anything on your behalf.

We publish aggregated, anonymized poll results — for example, "X%
of TeenCivics teens supported HR-1234." We do not publish your
individual vote linked to your identity.

## Gamification: Votes and tiers

The Votes you earn and the tier you reach are records of your
engagement with the site, intended for fun and motivation. They
have no monetary value, are not redeemable for prizes, and are not
transferable between accounts.

We reserve the right to adjust the tier ladder, daily caps, or
scoring rules. Major changes will be announced 30 days in advance
via email. We will not retroactively erase Votes you've earned
unless your account is found to have used bots or other prohibited
methods.

## Suspension and termination

We may suspend or terminate your account if:

- You violate these terms
- We believe your account is operated by an under-13 user
- Your account is being used to abuse other users
- Required by law

We will email you (using the address on your account) when we suspend
or terminate, with a brief explanation. You can appeal by replying.

You may terminate your own account at any time via `/account/delete`.

## Service availability

We try to keep TeenCivics running 24/7 but make no guarantees of
uptime. Civics education that you can read tomorrow is better than
no civics education at all, so we will prioritize correctness and
data integrity over instant availability if we ever have to choose.

We may take the site offline for maintenance, security incidents, or
extended outages. If down for more than 24 hours, we'll communicate
on @TeenCivics socials.

## Disclaimers

TeenCivics provides civics education content as-is. We work hard for
accuracy, but bill summaries are AI-generated and may contain errors.
For the authoritative text of any bill, refer to Congress.gov.

Nothing on TeenCivics constitutes legal advice, political advice, or
voting advice.

We don't endorse candidates, parties, or political positions, and
nothing on the site should be read as such.

## Changes to these terms

We will email registered users 30 days before any material change.
Non-material updates (typos, clarifications) take effect immediately
and are noted at the bottom of this page.

If you don't agree to a material change, you may close your account
before the change takes effect.

**For olivia (internal): Items the lawyer must rule on before launch:**

1. Whether mandatory arbitration is enforceable against under-18
   users in our target states. Many courts have refused to enforce
   it. If unenforceable, drop the section entirely rather than ship
   unenforceable language.
2. Governing law: California (where Liv is) vs Delaware (where many
   companies incorporate). Affects which state's consumer protection
   laws apply.
3. The CC license on our content — CC BY-NC-SA is the most common for
   educational nonprofits but locks out commercial reuse, which could
   affect future syndication deals. Some advisors recommend CC BY
   instead.
4. The "no bots on writes, free on reads" language is somewhat
   unusual. Confirm it's enforceable and matches our actual technical
   implementation (Cloudflare Turnstile on writes, open robots.txt
   for reads).
5. Whether the "we may suspend accounts of under-13 users" clause
   is sufficient COPPA defense if a 12-year-old slips through.
6. Whether the gamification language ("we reserve the right to
   adjust") creates any liability if we ever do a tier reset.
